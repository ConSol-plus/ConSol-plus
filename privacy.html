Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

ConSol+ ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, and the details you share about your situation when you contact us, book a consult, or work with us.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by a third-party payment processor. We do not store your full card number.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider and font provider may automatically receive your IP address and browser details when you visit.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your messages and schedule consults
  • To provide our consulting services and follow up with you
  • To process payments and keep business records
  • To improve our website and services

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (such as email, scheduling, payment, and hosting providers), when you ask us to share it with a referred professional, or when the law requires it.

Confidentiality

We keep what you tell us in a consult private and use it only to help you. Because we are not a law firm or licensed professional practice, our conversations are not protected by attorney-client or similar legal privilege.

Third-party services

Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. These providers have their own privacy policies and may collect technical information when you visit.

Data retention and your choices

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal or business requirements. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing essencial4us@gmail.com.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
