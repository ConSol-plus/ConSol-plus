How it works

  1. Pay and schedule your consult

    Reserve your spot by paying for a consult and picking a time that works for you.

  2. We gather the full picture

    You tell us about the problem, and we collect the surrounding details: what's happened so far, what's at stake, and what you want to see change.

  3. We map out your routes

    We work through everything and lay out your realistic options in plain language, so you can see the paths available and choose one.

  4. Follow through with support

    If you'd like, regular check-ins keep you accountable, and we adjust the plan as things change.

What we do and don't do

We're coaches and consultants. Being upfront about our limits protects you.

We do

  • Help you think through problems and decisions
  • Build budgets, plans, and goals with you
  • Teach general financial concepts and habits
  • Keep you accountable as you follow through
  • Refer you to licensed professionals

We don't

  • Recommend specific stocks, funds, or other securities
  • Give personalized investment, tax, or legal advice
  • Provide therapy, mental health treatment, or medical advice
  • Sell insurance, loans, or other regulated products
