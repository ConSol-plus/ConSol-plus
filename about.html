About ConSol+

ConSol+ is a general consulting service for people who are stuck on a problem and aren't sure where to turn. You bring the situation. We help you see the whole picture, lay out your realistic options, and decide what to do next.

Problems rarely fit in one box. A money worry is often tangled up with a job, a move, or a deadline. We look at everything around the problem, then work out routes that make sense for your life.

How we work
